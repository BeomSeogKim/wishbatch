From listening to making

A thoughtful path to a batch.

Less guesswork. A clear offer everyone can understand.

  1. 01 / LISTEN

    Gather the wishes.

    Fans say what they would like. AI groups compatible requests around formats you can produce.

  2. 02 / SHAPE

    Make the offer yours.

    You choose the product, price, minimum batch, and delivery terms. Fans authorize that exact offer through PayPal.

  3. 03 / CONFIRM

    Make when payments complete.

    Confirm production once enough units have holds. Only completed payments count toward funding the batch.
