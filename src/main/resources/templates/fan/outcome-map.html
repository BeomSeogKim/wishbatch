Where the money can go

PayPal sandbox demo: no real money moves. PayPal places the hold; The product does not hold funds.

  1. Authorize with PayPal
    • Authorized hold: no payment has been captured yet.
      • Enough authorized holds and maker confirmation → attempt capture. Capture can fail.
        • Completed capture → paid. Only completed payments count toward the paid minimum.
        • Capture shortfall → refund completed payments and release remaining holds.
      • Insufficient holds, maker cancellation, or an expired confirmation deadline → release remaining holds.
  2. Release of a hold
  3. Refund of a completed payment

Pending or failed release and refund attempts are not confirmed. Release and refund timing depends on PayPal and your bank or card.
