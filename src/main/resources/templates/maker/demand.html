How price limits are counted

Each wish counts once, regardless of requested quantity or repeated imports. Comparing wishes with minimum units is illustrative; it does not establish payment or production readiness.

Price limits are parsed from exact source quotes, never model-normalized amounts. The same server rule counts visible requests on every offer screen. A missing usable limit does not impose a price cap; it is not proof of willingness to pay.
